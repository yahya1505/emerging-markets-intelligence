# Dashboard Guide

## Page 1 — Risk Overview

**Purpose:** Identify countries with higher relative macroeconomic vulnerability in the selected peer group.

The page brings together headline risk metrics, country rankings, geographic distribution, and a country-by-component comparison. The risk score is relative to the model and peer group, not a crisis probability.

## Page 2 — Country Risk Drivers

**Purpose:** Understand the component profile of a selected country and compare it with the peer group.

Use the country selector to inspect the overall score, relative rank, component scores, peer benchmarks, and risk implications. Implications are prompts for additional analysis, not causal conclusions.

## Page 3 — Historical Economic Trends

**Purpose:** Explore annual indicator movements from 2005–2024 and compare countries over time.

The page includes trend and cross-country comparison views. If the line chart becomes crowded, reduce the number of selected countries. Heatmap colors are indicator-specific display cues and are separate from the composite risk score.

## Validation checks before publication

- Test country, continent, indicator, and year slicers.
- Confirm the indicator slicer filters the historical fact values correctly.
- Check visual interactions and tooltips.
- Confirm that the units are visible and consistent.
- Treat missing values as missing, not as zero.
- Add genuine screenshots from the current report to the `screenshots/` directory.
